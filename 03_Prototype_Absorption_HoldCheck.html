What's new vs today's Absorption indicator
  • Classic (desktop, Global+) marks any price where traded volume within a window passes a threshold. It keeps counting after price trades through, so it can't tell a level that held from one that broke. Its alerts need setup (a self-built Telegram bot), and there are no ES/CL presets.
  • Hold-check alerts only when the level survives a short confirm window and shows evidence it refilled. It tolerates a 1-tick poke if price reclaims the level, then follows each event to Held or Broken. ES/CL presets, one sensitivity control, alerts that work without setup, and presets credited to their author.

Alerts §6.5
  • Absorbing alerts by default; Reacting, Held and Broken are opt-in. Alerts cover every open instrument (watch the CL tab dot), capped at 3 per minute per instrument, and only inside the preset's alert hours.
  • MVP (web) alerts go through an in-app toast, sound and browser notification. Desktop parity (Phase 2) reuses Bookmap's central alert system (pop-up, sound, Alerts History). App in background shows the OS/browser notification.
Presets & attribution §6.3, §6.8
  • The partner's "[Partner Educator] ES/CL" templates ship built in, pre-filled with per-instrument starting values so they work with zero setup, and are credited "by [Partner Educator] ✓". Bookmap verifies who published a preset, not its results. Sharing by file or code for other educators is S.
Data modes §6.1, §6.11
  • MBP works on any feed with aggressor-side trades. MBO (Phase 2) adds order-level facts to the tooltip. No aggressor flag shows the unavailable state.
Web vs Desktop §7
  • Web (MVP): a new "Absorption" algorithm/widget inside Market Pulse Web, reusing its add-widget flow and per-widget sound thresholds. Detection runs server-side (one engine per instrument, shared by every viewer), so alerts don't depend on a desktop app being open. Browser notifications need permission, and sound needs a first click.
  • Desktop (Phase 2, parity): the same server-side logic surfaces in today's Absorption indicator. Settings live in Settings › Configure add-ons; alerts go through File › Alerts, with native notifications that keep working while minimised.

About

Classic (today) marks a price when traded volume within a window passes a threshold. It keeps counting after price trades through, so it can't tell a level that held from one that broke.

Hold-check counts only while the level stands. It alerts only after a short confirm window with evidence the level refilled or kept its visible size, allows a brief 1-tick poke that reclaims, and then follows the event to Held or Broken.

What it doesn't do: predict reversals, place orders, or rate trades. Held and Broken record what price did afterwards.

Classic stays available and keeps publishing to Market Pulse "Absorption Pressure" unchanged. This prototype doesn't simulate Classic itself; the ◆ comparison marks approximate it.
